# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Cottage home bakers who sell baked goods and need to manage the business side of their work.

## Product Purpose

KneadIt helps home bakers run their bakery business in one place, so they can spend less time coordinating orders, money, customers, and operations manually.

## Positioning

KneadIt is business management software built specifically for cottage food bakers, combining bakery-specific workflows such as recipes, orders, storefronts, invoicing, customer relationships, and finances.

## Operating Context

Users run their businesses from a home kitchen using a phone, tablet, or laptop. Their work follows a recurring rhythm of taking orders, preparing and baking products, collecting payment, fulfilling orders, and reviewing business performance.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Orders and order status workflows
- Customer-facing storefronts
- Invoicing and payment tracking, including PayPal integration
- Recipes and recipe costing
- Finance and profit tracking
- Customer reviews, communications, and marketing support
- Three subscription plans and a 30-day free trial
- The platform runs in a browser; no special hardware or app installation is required
- Users remain responsible for compliance with their state's cottage food laws

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: KneadIt
- Existing KneadIt logo and bakery-inspired identity remain recognizable
- Voice should be practical, encouraging, and grounded in the real work of home baking

- Make the business side feel manageable.
- Reflect the real rhythm of a home bakery.

- Give bakers clarity without requiring business software expertise.
