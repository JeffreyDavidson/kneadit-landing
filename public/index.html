Is KneadIt for a bakery like mine?

It is built for cottage food and home bakery businesses, whether you sell custom cakes, weekly bread batches, cookies, or a mix of your favorites.

Do I need to install anything?

No. KneadIt runs in your browser on a phone, tablet, or computer.

How do payments work?

Customers pay you directly. KneadIt offers payment tracking and PayPal invoicing on eligible plans. Your payment provider’s fees may apply.

Can I try it before I commit?

Yes. Every plan starts with a 30-day free trial, and no credit card is required.
