# KneadIt Landing Design Notes

The landing page uses KneadIt's warm bakery palette, DM Sans body type, Manrope
display type, editorial spacing, and a paper-and-forest visual language. The
standalone project owns presentation only; product behavior and customer data
remain in the KneadIt application.

## Principles

- Make the business side feel manageable.
- Show the relationship between orders, customers, recipes, invoices, and profit.
- Keep calls to action clear and calm.
- Preserve keyboard focus, readable contrast, and reduced-motion behavior.
- Keep application URLs configurable as the deployment boundary is finalized.
